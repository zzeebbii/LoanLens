## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? What constraints apply? State the facts,
including anything that was measured or verified.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. One paragraph.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder. Be honest about the cost — an ADR that lists only
benefits is not recording a decision, it is advertising one.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and the specific reason each was not chosen.
